Questions parents ask

Can we try a class first?

Yes. Join the ₹49 Masterclass (Maths Sun 22 Nov, Science Sun 29 Nov), or join a free weekly live class on our YouTube channel (Maths Sundays, Science Saturdays, 7–8 pm). If you then join a batch, the ₹49 is adjusted in the fee.

Are the live classes taught by AI?

No. Every live class is taught by a real teacher, and the long answers in every mock are marked by that teacher. AI is used only for our free YouTube revision videos.

When are the board exams?

CBSE has not released the 2027 date sheet yet; Class 10 exams are expected to begin around mid-February. Batch 1 ends on 16 Jan. A shorter crash-course batch is planned for 18 Jan to 13 Feb, finishing before the expected start, with a free doubt clinic before each paper.

My child takes Maths Basic. Can they join?

The Maths batch follows Maths Standard. Message us; if enough Basic students ask, we'll open a separate batch.

What if my child misses a class?

Every class is recorded and shared in the batch group the same night.

Do you offer one-to-one tuition?

Yes, for students who need individual attention. Message us and we'll match you with a teacher.

What do I need?

A laptop, tablet or phone with a stable internet connection, a notebook, and the NCERT textbooks.
