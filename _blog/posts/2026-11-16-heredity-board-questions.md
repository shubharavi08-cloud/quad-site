Chapter 8 of CBSE Class 10 Science, *Heredity*, is short, but it carries some of the easiest marks in the paper if you can draw a **Punnett square** without panicking. Almost every question is one of three things: a **Mendel cross** (one trait or two), a question on **inherited versus acquired traits**, or **sex determination**.

## The two ratios you must know first

| Cross | F₁ | F₂ phenotype ratio | F₂ genotype ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monohybrid (one trait), e.g. TT × tt | All Tt (dominant look) | **3 : 1** | **1 : 2 : 1** (TT : Tt : tt) |
| Dihybrid (two traits), e.g. RRYY × rryy | All RrYy | **9 : 3 : 3 : 1** | (not asked at Class 10 level) |

Write the **parents**, then the **gametes**, then the **square**. Examiners give a mark for each step.

## Type 1: The F₂ genotype ratio (1 mark)

> A green-stemmed tomato plant (GG) is crossed with a purple-stemmed plant (gg). The hybrid is self-pollinated. What is the ratio of GG, Gg and gg in F₂? (a) 2 : 1 : 1 (b) 3 : 1 : 0 (c) 1 : 1 : 2 (d) 1 : 2 : 1

- GG × gg gives F₁ **all Gg**.
- Gg × Gg: each parent makes G and g gametes, so the square holds **GG, Gg, Gg, gg**.
- **Answer: (d) 1 : 2 : 1.**

**Trap:** choosing 3 : 1 : 0. That mixes up the *phenotype* ratio (3 green : 1 purple) with the *genotype* ratio.

**Try it:** what fraction of the F₂ plants have purple stems? **1/4 (only gg).**

## Type 2: New combinations in a dihybrid cross (1 mark)

> Round green (RRyy) × wrinkled yellow (rrYY) gives F₁ round yellow (RrYy). On selfing F₁, which **new** combinations appear in F₂? (i) round yellow (ii) round green (iii) wrinkled yellow (iv) wrinkled green

- F₂ shows all four combinations.
- Round green and wrinkled yellow are the **parents' own** looks.
- Round yellow and wrinkled green are **new**. **Answer: (i) and (iv).**

This is the evidence for **independent inheritance**: the seed-shape and seed-colour traits are passed on separately.

**Trap:** picking (ii) and (iii), which are exactly the parental combinations.

## Type 3: Which chromosomes does the zygote get? (1 mark)

> A human egg is fertilised by a sperm carrying a Y chromosome. The zygote has: (a) 44 + XX (b) 22 + XX (c) 44 + XY (d) 22 + XY

- Egg: **22 + X**. Sperm with Y: **22 + Y**.
- Zygote: **44 + XY**, which develops into a **boy**. **Answer: (c).**

**Trap:** choosing 22 + XY. A zygote has the **full set**: 22 pairs of autosomes (44) plus one pair of sex chromosomes.

**Try it:** what does an X-carrying sperm give with an egg? **44 + XX, a girl.**

## Type 4: Traits that cannot be inherited (1 mark)

> Which cannot be passed from parents to offspring? (i) swimming technique (ii) blood group (iii) eye colour (iv) dancing skills

- **Inherited traits** are controlled by genes in DNA: blood group, eye colour.
- **Acquired traits** are learnt or developed during life: swimming, dancing. They do not change the DNA of the germ cells, so they are **not passed on**. **Answer: (i) and (iv).**

**Try it:** is a scar from an injury inherited? **No, it is an acquired trait.**

## Type 5: Red and white pea flowers, 33 : 11 (3 marks)

> Red-flowered × white-flowered pea plants: all 30 F₁ plants are red. Of 44 F₂ plants, 33 are red and 11 are white. (a) Show the cross. (b) Give the F₂ phenotype and genotype ratios.

- All F₁ red, so **red is dominant (R)** and white is recessive (r).
- Parents **RR × rr** → F₁ **all Rr** (red).
- F₁ selfed: **Rr × Rr** → RR, Rr, Rr, rr.
- **Phenotype ratio 3 red : 1 white** (33 : 11 = 3 : 1). **Genotype ratio 1 RR : 2 Rr : 1 rr.**

**Trap:** writing only "33 : 11". The marking scheme wants the **ratio 3 : 1** and the genotypes 1 : 2 : 1.

**Try it:** of the 33 red F₂ plants, about how many are pure-breeding (RR)? **About 11, one-third.**

## Type 6: Is sex always decided by genes? (3 marks)

> (a) Give two examples to show that the sex of the offspring is not always genetically determined in animals. (b) Differentiate between the sex chromosomes of human males and females.

- (a) In **some reptiles**, the **temperature** at which the fertilised eggs are kept decides the sex. In **some snails**, an individual can **change sex** during its life.
- (b) Women have a **perfect pair, XX**. Men have a **mismatched pair, XY**, where Y is shorter.
- In humans, the child's sex depends on whether the **father's sperm** carries X or Y; the egg always carries X. The chance of a boy or a girl is **50 : 50**.

**Trap:** giving human examples for part (a). The question asks for animals where sex is **not** decided by genes.

## Type 7: Tall purple × short white, up to F₂ (3 marks)

> Tall (T) is dominant over short (t); purple flowers (P) over white (p). Cross a plant pure for both dominant traits with one recessive for both and work out F₂. What is the probability of tall plants with white flowers?

- Parents **TTPP × ttpp** → F₁ **all TtPp** (tall, purple).
- F₁ gametes: **TP, Tp, tP, tp**; draw the 4 × 4 square of 16 boxes.
- F₂: **9 tall purple : 3 tall white : 3 short purple : 1 short white**.
- Probability of tall white = **3/16**.

**Trap:** writing 1/16. That is short white, where *both* traits are recessive. Tall white has one dominant trait, so it is 3/16.

**Try it:** probability of short plants with purple flowers? **3/16.**

## Five-minute revision checklist

- Monohybrid F₂: phenotype 3 : 1, genotype 1 : 2 : 1. Dihybrid F₂: 9 : 3 : 3 : 1.
- If every F₁ plant shows one trait, that trait is dominant.
- New combinations in a dihybrid F₂ prove independent inheritance.
- Inherited traits come from genes; skills and injuries are acquired and not passed on.
- Humans: 22 pairs of autosomes + 1 pair of sex chromosomes; female XX, male XY; the sperm decides the sex.
- Reptiles (egg temperature) and snails (sex change): sex not always genetic.

## FAQ

**Is Heredity important for the CBSE board exam?**
Yes. Expect at least one MCQ on a ratio or on 44 + XY, and a 3-mark question asking you to show a cross. It is one of the quickest chapters to revise fully.

**How do I avoid mistakes in a Punnett square?**
Always write the gametes of each parent along the top and side before you fill the boxes, then count each type. Convert actual numbers (like 33 : 11) into the simplest ratio.
